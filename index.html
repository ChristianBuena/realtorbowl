Realtor Bowl is a Fantasy Football league designed just for real estate professionals in the state of California. Whether you're closing deals or closing out the competition, the Realtor Bowl is the place to test your football knowledge while networking with the best in the business.

Each team manager picks their roster from real NFL players and earns points based on their real-life performance each week. Manage your team, make trades, pick up free agents, and battle it out for weekly matchups and the ultimate championship title!

The game is powered by ESPN Fantasy Football — so you get a world-class platform for drafting, scoring, and tracking your season progress.
